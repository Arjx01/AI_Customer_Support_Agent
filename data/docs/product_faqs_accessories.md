# Product FAQs: Smart Accessories and Peripherals

## Logitech MX Master Wireless Mouse
### Specifications and Features
- **Sensor**: Darkfield high precision 8000 DPI sensor capable of tracking on any surface, including glass.
- **Scroll Wheel**: MagSpeed Electromagnetic scrolling shifts from ratchet to hyper-fast scrolling at 1000 lines per second.
- **Multi-Device Connectivity**: Pair with up to 3 devices via Bluetooth Low Energy or Logi Bolt USB receiver; switch seamlessly with Easy-Switch button.
- **Battery Life**: Rechargeable 500mAh Li-Po battery lasts up to 70 days on a full charge; 1-minute quick charge gives 3 hours of use.

## Samsung Galaxy Watch
### Health Tracking and Battery
- **Health Sensors**: BioActive Sensor measuring optical heart rate, electrical cardiac signal (ECG), and bioelectrical impedance analysis (BIA for body composition).
- **Sleep Coaching**: Advanced sleep monitoring with snore detection and personalized sleep scores.
- **Durability**: Sapphire Crystal glass face with 5ATM + IP68 water resistance rating and MIL-STD-810H compliance.
- **Operating System**: Wear OS Powered by Samsung with access to Google Play Store apps.

## HP Smart Tank Wireless Printer
### Setup and Maintenance
- **High-Yield Inktank**: Up to 6,000 black pages or 8,000 color pages included with initial spill-free ink bottles.
- **Connectivity**: Dual-band Wi-Fi with self-healing capabilities, Wi-Fi Direct, and Hi-Speed USB 2.0.
- **Mobile Printing**: HP Smart App enables remote printing, mobile faxing, and high-resolution scanning directly to smartphones.
- **Print Speed**: Up to 12 ppm for black documents and 5 ppm for color.
