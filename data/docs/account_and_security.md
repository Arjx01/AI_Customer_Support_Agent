# Account Management and Security

## Managing Your Account Profile
- **Updating Email or Phone Number**: Go to Profile Settings > Personal Information. Changing your primary phone number requires OTP verification on both old and new numbers.
- **Managing Delivery Addresses**: You can save up to 10 shipping addresses. Ensure your address has an accurate 6-digit pin code and landmark to avoid courier delays.

## Password and Account Security
- **Strong Password Requirements**: Passwords must be at least 8 characters long and include an uppercase letter, a number, and a special symbol.
- **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**: We strongly recommend enabling Two-Factor Authentication via SMS OTP or an Authenticator App (Google Authenticator, Authy).
- **Resetting Forgotten Passwords**: Click "Forgot Password" on the sign-in page to receive a password reset link valid for 15 minutes.

## Privacy and Data Protection
- We adhere strictly to data privacy standards and 256-bit encryption for all stored sensitive data.
- Payment card details are tokenized in compliance with RBI guidelines; CVVs and complete card numbers are never stored on our servers.
- To request account closure or data erasure, reach out to our privacy compliance desk through support.
