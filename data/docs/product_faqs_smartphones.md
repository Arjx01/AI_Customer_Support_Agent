# Product FAQs: Smartphones

## iPhone 16
### Technical Specifications and Features
- **Display**: 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR OLED with Ceramic Shield front.
- **Processor**: A18 Bionic chip with 6-core CPU and 16-core Neural Engine.
- **Camera**: 48MP Fusion main camera with 2x telephoto optical-quality zoom and 12MP Ultra Wide with macro photography.
- **Battery**: Up to 22 hours of video playback. Fast USB-C charging up to 50% in 30 minutes with a 20W adapter (sold separately).
- **Storage Options**: 128GB, 256GB, and 512GB.

### What is in the box?
- iPhone 16 with iOS 18
- USB-C Charge Cable (1m)
- Documentation and SIM ejector pin (Power adapter is sold separately).

## Samsung Galaxy S24
### Key Features and Galaxy AI
- **Display**: 6.2-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X with 1-120Hz adaptive refresh rate and 2600 nits peak brightness.
- **Processor**: Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 for Galaxy (or Exynos 2400 depending on region).
- **Camera**: Triple rear camera setup: 50MP Wide, 10MP 3x Optical Zoom Telephoto, and 12MP Ultra-Wide.
- **AI Features**: Circle to Search with Google, Live Call Translation, Note Assist, and Photo Assist.
- **Water Resistance**: IP68 dust and water resistance (up to 1.5m for 30 minutes).

## OnePlus 13
### Performance and Battery
- **Display**: 6.82-inch 2K 120Hz ProXDR display with Dolby Vision.
- **Battery & Charging**: 6000mAh battery supporting 100W SUPERVOOC wired fast charging and 50W AIRVOOC wireless charging.
- **Camera System**: Hasselblad 4th Gen camera with 50MP Sony LYT-808 primary sensor.
- **Operating System**: OxygenOS 15 based on Android 15.
