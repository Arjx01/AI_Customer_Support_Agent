# Troubleshooting Guide: Common Hardware and Device Issues

## Smartphone Troubleshooting
### Device Not Turning On or Frozen Screen
1. Connect the phone to the official original charger and cable; let it charge for at least 20 minutes.
2. Perform a **Force Restart**:
   - **iPhone**: Press and quickly release Volume Up, press and quickly release Volume Down, then hold Side Power button until the Apple logo appears.
   - **Samsung / Android**: Press and hold Volume Down and Power button simultaneously for 10-15 seconds until the device vibrates and restarts.

### Battery Draining Rapidly
- Check Settings > Battery to identify battery-draining apps.
- Reduce screen brightness, enable Dark Mode, and set Screen Timeout to 30 seconds.
- Turn off 5G Auto or Always-On Display if you are in poor network reception zones.

## Bluetooth and Audio Troubleshooting
### Headphones or Earbuds Not Connecting
1. Turn off Bluetooth on your phone/laptop, wait 10 seconds, and turn it back on.
2. "Forget This Device" in your phone's Bluetooth settings and re-pair from scratch.
3. Verify the headphones are in active pairing mode (status LED flashing rapidly).
4. Perform a factory reset on the audio device as described in the audio product manual.

## Computer Peripherals and Wi-Fi Printer Issues
### Wireless Mouse Lagging or Disconnecting
- Replace or recharge the internal battery.
- Ensure the USB dongle is connected directly to a computer port rather than an unpowered USB hub.
- Move the mouse closer to the receiver to minimize 2.4GHz Wi-Fi interference.

### Printer Offline or Not Printing
- Restart both your Wi-Fi router and the printer.
- Verify that both your computer and the printer are connected to the exact same 2.4GHz network SSID.
- Check ink levels and clear any paper jam from the input tray or rear door.
