# Product FAQs: Audio Devices

## Sony WH-1000XM5 Wireless Headphones
### Features and Active Noise Cancellation (ANC)
- **Noise Cancellation**: Dual processors (Integrated Processor V1 and HD Noise Cancelling Processor QN1) with 8 microphones for industry-leading ANC.
- **Audio Quality**: High-Resolution Audio support via LDAC codec, DSEE Extreme upscaling, and 30mm carbon fiber drivers.
- **Battery Life**: Up to 30 hours with ANC enabled; 3-minute quick charge yields 3 hours of playback.
- **Smart Listening**: Speak-to-Chat automatically pauses playback when you start speaking; wearing sensor detects when headphones are removed.

### How to reset Sony WH-1000XM5
- Connect the headphones to a USB power source using the supplied USB-C cable.
- Press and hold both the Power button and the NC/AMB button simultaneously for about 7 seconds.
- The blue LED indicator will flash 4 times to indicate complete factory reset.

## Apple AirPods Pro (2nd Generation)
### Features and Capabilities
- **Processor**: Apple H2 chip providing 2x more Active Noise Cancellation than previous generation.
- **Audio Technology**: Adaptive Audio, Transparency mode, and Personalized Spatial Audio with dynamic head tracking.
- **Charging Case**: MagSafe Charging Case (USB-C) with built-in speaker for Find My alerts and lanyard loop.
- **Battery Life**: Up to 6 hours of listening time with ANC enabled, and up to 30 hours of total listening time with the case.

### How to pair or reset AirPods Pro
- Place AirPods in the charging case and keep the lid open.
- Press and hold the setup button on the back of the case for about 15 seconds until the status light flashes amber, then white.
- Bring the open case next to your iPhone or iPad and follow the on-screen setup prompt.
