# Return and Refund Policy

## Overview
At our store, customer satisfaction is our top priority. We offer a transparent, customer-friendly return and refund process for all eligible purchases.

## Refund Window
- You may request a refund within **30 days of the delivery date**.
- Orders older than 30 days from delivery are outside the return window and cannot be refunded.

## Eligibility Requirements
To qualify for a refund, your order must meet the following criteria:
1. **Delivered Status**: The item must have been successfully marked as 'Delivered'. Orders that are 'In Transit', 'Processing', or 'Not Dispatched' are not yet eligible for return refunds (if you wish to cancel an unfulfilled order, please contact customer support).
2. **Valid Order and Payment**: Original payment must have been successful. Cancelled orders are already marked as cancelled/refunded and cannot be re-refunded.
3. **Refundable Balance**: The requested refund amount cannot exceed the remaining refundable balance of the order.
4. **Item Condition**: Physical products must be in their original packaging with all included accessories, manuals, and warranty cards.

## Full and Partial Refunds
- **Full Refunds**: If you return the entire order, you will receive a 100% refund of the purchase price.
- **Partial Refunds**: If you only returned part of a multi-item shipment or received a partially damaged/discounted item, you can request a partial refund.
- **Multiple Partial Refunds**: You may request multiple partial refunds over time, provided the cumulative refunded amount does not exceed the total price paid for the order.

## Non-Refundable Categories
The following categories and items are strictly non-refundable:
- Digital gift cards and prepaid vouchers.
- Downloadable software licenses and digital media keys.
- Custom-engraved or personalized electronic accessories.
- Final clearance items explicitly marked "Final Sale".

## Refund Processing and Method
- All refunds are credited directly back to the **original payment method** used at checkout (UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, or Net Banking).
- Our support agent will verify your eligibility and ask for your explicit confirmation of the refund amount before processing.
- Once confirmed, the refund transaction is generated immediately with a unique Refund ID. Funds typically reflect in your account within 3 to 5 business days depending on your bank or payment provider.
