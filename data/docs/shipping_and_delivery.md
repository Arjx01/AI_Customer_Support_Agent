# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Delivery Timelines and Options
We offer reliable shipping across all major regions:
- **Standard Shipping**: Typically delivered within 3 to 5 business days after dispatch.
- **Express Shipping**: Delivered within 1 to 2 business days for select metropolitan pin codes.
- **Same-Day Delivery**: Available for orders placed before 11:00 AM in eligible urban zones.

## Tracking Your Package
- Once your order is dispatched, you will receive an automated tracking link and SMS update.
- You can check the current status of your shipment anytime by providing your Order ID (e.g., `ORD1001`) to our customer support agent.
- Common delivery statuses include:
  - **Not Dispatched**: Your order is being packed and prepared at our warehouse.
  - **In Transit**: Your package is with the courier partner and on its way to your destination hub.
  - **Out for Delivery**: The courier agent is delivering your package today.
  - **Delivered**: The parcel has been safely handed over to the recipient.
  - **Returned to Seller**: Delivery failed after 3 attempts or was rejected, and the parcel was returned to our warehouse.

## Courier Partners
We partner with top-tier logistics providers including Blue Dart, Delhivery, DTDC, and FedEx to ensure fast and safe transit of high-value electronic goods.

## Shipping Charges
- Standard shipping is **free** on all orders over ₹499.
- Orders below ₹499 incur a flat nominal shipping fee of ₹49.
- Express priority delivery incurs an additional ₹99 surcharge.

## Damaged or Missing Shipments
If your package arrives visibly damaged or tampered with, please take photos before opening and notify customer support within 48 hours of delivery. If tracking shows delivered but you have not received your parcel, please contact our support agent immediately for investigation.
