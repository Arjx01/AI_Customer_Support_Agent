# Warranty and Repairs Policy

## Standard Manufacturer Warranty
All electronics and hardware products sold on our platform come with a **1-Year Manufacturer Warranty** starting from the confirmed date of delivery.
- **Coverage**: Covers manufacturing defects, hardware component failures, screen display anomalies (dead pixels beyond threshold), power issues, and internal circuitry malfunctions.
- **Exclusions**: Physical damage caused by drops or impact, liquid spills/immersion (unless rated waterproof and within specification limits), unauthorized disassembly, software rooting/jailbreaking, and normal aesthetic wear-and-tear.

## Warranty Claim Process
1. Locate your tax invoice and serial number on the original packaging or in your order details.
2. Contact our AI customer support agent or visit the authorized brand service center (Apple, Samsung, Dell, Sony, Logitech, HP, OnePlus).
3. The service center will inspect the device and provide free repair or replacement of defective parts within the warranty tenure.

## Extended Warranty & Accidental Damage Protection
- Extended warranty plans (covering Year 2 and Year 3) must be purchased within 15 days of product delivery.
- Accidental Damage Protection (ADP) covers screen cracking and liquid damage with a nominal deductible per incident.

## Replacement Policy
If a newly delivered hardware item is dead-on-arrival (DOA) or exhibits a verifiable defect within **7 days of delivery**, our team will arrange a doorstep replacement or instant return rather than requiring repair.
