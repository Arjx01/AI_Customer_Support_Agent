# Product FAQs: Laptops and Computing

## MacBook Air M3
### Overview and Key Capabilities
- **Processor**: Apple M3 chip with an 8-core CPU, up to 10-core GPU, and hardware-accelerated ray tracing.
- **Battery Life**: Up to 18 hours of battery life with silent, fanless design.
- **Display**: 13.6-inch Liquid Retina display with 500 nits brightness and True Tone technology.
- **External Display Support**: Supports up to two external displays when the laptop lid is closed.
- **Ports & Audio**: MagSafe 3 charging port, two Thunderbolt 4 / USB 4 ports, 3.5mm headphone jack, 1080p FaceTime HD camera.

## Dell Ultrasharp Monitor
### Display Specifications and Connectivity
- **Screen Size & Resolution**: 27-inch 4K UHD (3840 x 2160) IPS panel with 99% sRGB color gamut and HDR support.
- **Connectivity**: USB-C Hub with 90W Power Delivery (charges laptops over single cable), HDMI 2.1, DisplayPort 1.4, and 4x USB-A downstream ports.
- **Ergonomics**: Height, tilt, swivel, and pivot adjustable stand. VESA 100x100mm wall-mount compatible.

## Mechanical Keyboard
### Switches and Compatibility
- **Switch Type**: Hot-swappable custom tactile mechanical switches rated for 80 million keystrokes.
- **Connectivity Modes**: Bluetooth 5.1, 2.4GHz wireless dongle (1000Hz polling rate), and detachable USB-C wired connection.
- **Operating System Compatibility**: Full native support for Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android with a dedicated layout toggle switch.
- **Backlighting**: Per-key RGB with 22 dynamic animation modes.
