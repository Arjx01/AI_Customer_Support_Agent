# Payment Methods and Billing

## Supported Payment Methods
We support a wide array of safe, encrypted payment options:
- **Unified Payments Interface (UPI)**: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and bank UPI apps. Instant settlement with zero transaction charges.
- **Credit and Debit Cards**: Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, and American Express. Secured via 256-bit SSL encryption and Two-Factor OTP authentication.
- **Net Banking**: Directly supported across 50+ nationalized and private banking institutions.
- **EMI Options**: No-cost EMI and low-interest EMI plans available on major credit cards for purchases over ₹3,000.

## Payment Status Explanations
Your order billing state may reflect one of the following payment statuses:
- **Successful**: Payment was authorized, captured, and confirmed by the payment gateway.
- **Pending**: Payment is awaiting bank clearance or OTP verification.
- **Failed**: Transaction declined by the bank or gateway timeout; no funds are deducted.
- **Partially Refunded**: A portion of the original transaction has been credited back to the customer.
- **Refunded**: The full purchase value has been refunded back to the originating account.

## Invoices and Receipts
- GST-compliant tax invoices are automatically generated upon order confirmation.
- You can request an invoice copy from our AI support agent by specifying your Order ID.
- Invoices detail the product serial numbers, applicable GST rates (18% standard on electronics), and shipping details.

## Billing Discrepancies and Duplicate Debits
If your account was debited but the order shows 'Pending' or 'Failed', banks usually reverse the charge automatically within 24 to 48 hours. If the amount is not reversed within 3 business days, provide the transaction reference number (UTR or RRN) to our support team for prompt reconciliation.
